# Thinksy

Thinksy is an exploration of experimental reasoning through interactive systems. Exploring how players can discover hidden rules, understand system behaviour, and predict outcomes through experimentation, pattern recognition, and observation. The game focuses on deterministic systems where inputs, outputs, and previous interactions reveal how things work.

## What This Covers

The work follows the complete product development lifecycle, from initial research to building, testing, and improving the game.

1. **Problem Discovery & Research:** Understand the problem, target players, market, player needs, existing games, and opportunities in experimental reasoning.
2. **Product Strategy & Definition:** Define the product vision, goals, value proposition, gameplay principles, requirements, MVP scope, and roadmap.
3. **Product Design & User Experience:** Map player journeys, gameplay loops, challenge structures, workflows, wireframes, prototypes, and interface designs.
4. **Technical Research & System Architecture:** Define technical feasibility, technology stack, game engine, system architecture, data models, APIs, and integrations.
5. **Engineering & Implementation:** Build the game, implement core mechanics, develop the underlying systems, manage code, and document technical decisions.
6. **Testing, Validation & Quality Assurance:** Test gameplay, verify deterministic behaviour, identify issues, evaluate challenge solvability, and validate the player experience.
7. **Business Model & Commercial Strategy:** Explore the business model, target audience, market positioning, monetisation, pricing, and commercial viability.
8. **Go-to-Market, Marketing & Distribution:** Define audience acquisition, product messaging, brand positioning, marketing assets, distribution channels, and launch strategy.
9. **Deployment & Release:** Prepare the production environment, configure deployment, establish monitoring, and manage product releases.
10. **Open Source & Developer Documentation:** Prepare the README, installation guide, architecture documentation, API references, licensing, troubleshooting, and contribution guidelines.
11. **Results, Evaluation & Iteration:** Evaluate gameplay, product performance, player feedback, engagement, measure outcomes, and plan future improvements.
12. **Project Case Study & Publication:** Document the project journey, demonstrate the game, publish project details, and record outcomes and learnings.
